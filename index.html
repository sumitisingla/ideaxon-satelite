The Challenge

Sugarcane Feeds an Industry.
Its Farms Still Run on Guesswork.

Cane is a long, water-hungry, high-value crop. Many small decisions over 12 to 18 months decide both the farmer's income and the mill's sugar recovery.

01

Water Stress

Flood irrigation wastes water, and groundwater keeps falling across the major cane belts.

02

Pests & Disease

Red rot, shoot and top borer, pyrilla and woolly aphid are spotted too late to contain.

03

Harvest Delays

Cane that's cut past its peak, or left waiting at the mill gate, loses weight and sucrose every day.

04

Information Gaps

Farmers, field staff and mills still rely on paper slips, phone calls and late payment updates.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Do mills or farmers need to buy sensors or hardware?

No. Ideaxon runs on satellite data, weather data and expert advisories, so cooperative sugar mills and farmers carry no sensor or installation cost.

Do farmers need a smartphone?

A smartphone gives the full app experience. Farmers without one still get SMS and voice-call advisories in their language, and mill field officers or FPO staff can manage plots on their behalf.

How is "up to 50% more income" achieved?

It comes from a mix of higher yield, lower spending on water, fertiliser and sprays, fewer crop losses to pests and weather, and harvesting at peak recovery. Results vary by farm, and 50% is our upper target.

How does it help sugar mills?

Mills get a live view of every registered plot in their command area, including acreage, crop stage, maturity and expected tonnage. They can plan cutting orders, harvesters and transport for a steady crushing season with better recovery.

What do state governments get?

Satellite-verified sugarcane area, crop-stage and stress maps by district, and early production estimates that support planning, procurement and scheme delivery.

Who owns the farm data?

The farmer does. Data is shared with mills, FPOs, lenders or government only with the farmer's consent, and it's stored securely in India.
